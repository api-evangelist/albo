# Albo

> Albo (albo.com, formerly albo.mx) is a Mexican digital financial services company
> founded in 2016. Since 2022 it operates as an Institución de Fondos de Pago Electrónico
> (IFPE) authorized by the Comisión Nacional Bancaria y de Valores (CNBV) and regulated by
> Banco de México. Albo ships a mobile-first personal account with a Mastercard debit card
> (SPEI transfers, bill payments, cash deposit/withdrawal at partner locations, real-time
> notifications) and "albo empresa", a business account for payroll, employee cards and
> bulk dispersion of up to 3,000 transactions at a time.

## Status of the developer surface

Albo publishes no public developer program. As of 2026-08-06 there is no developer portal,
no API reference, no OpenAPI / AsyncAPI / GraphQL specification, no SDK or client library
on any public package registry, no webhook catalog, no public Postman collection, and no
sandbox. Every developer-shaped hostname probed (api., developers., developer., docs.,
business., negocios. on both albo.com and albo.mx) fails to resolve. Albo's products are
end-user apps; integration with Albo accounts is reached, when at all, through third-party
open-banking aggregators rather than a first-party Albo API.

## Products

- [albo personal](https://www.albo.com/albo/personal.html): Digital account + Mastercard debit card, SPEI transfers, bill payments, cash in/out
- [albo empresa](https://www.albo.com/empresa/home.html): Business account, payroll, employee cards, bulk dispersion (up to 3,000 transactions at once)
- [abea](https://www.abea.mx/): Personal credit — independent partner (Aureo Lab), not an Albo product
- [albit](https://www.albit.app/): Crypto buy/sell/transfer — independent partner (Indigo DeFi), not an Albo product
